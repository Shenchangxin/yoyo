# Harness / RSI

Overrides MASTER for the laboratory workspace.

- Process rail: Overview (unnumbered origin) then `01 Propose` `02 Prove` `03 Promote`. Underline tab, not fill pills.
- Next action is the only headline on Overview (≤15px/600). Verb on the right.
- Pointers are one hairline list. Active is trusted; staging/canary/HEAD are labels on the same objects.
- Lineage is a vertical step rail, not a stack of cards.
- Harbor/Evolve toolbars are one instrument strip: primary verb + overflow. Gates are pressed chips, not a checkbox farm.
