# Episode outline

Work on one planned episode, not the whole novel.

1. Read only that window (`drama_read_episode` after commit, or `drama_read_source` with its start/end before commit).
2. State the on-screen goal, the external obstacle, the internal flaw, the midpoint reversal.
3. End on a visible hook, not a theme statement.
4. Keep A/B lines and locations short enough for 45–90 seconds of finished film.

Do not write the shooting script here. Do not generate images or video.
