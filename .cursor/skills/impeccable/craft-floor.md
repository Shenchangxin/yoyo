# Craft floor

Load this after direction is settled. A pinned brief overrides anything here.

## Verify

- **Contrast:** body and placeholder ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1.
- **Depth:** shadows have offset and soft blur. A zero-offset colored halo is decoration.
- **Spacing:** tight groups, generous separation, more space above a heading than below it.
- **Type:** body 65–75ch; obvious scale and weight steps; balanced headings.
- **Motion:** one authored moment, not scattered section entrances. Exponential ease-out.
- **States:** hover, disabled, loading, error, empty. Keyboard focus. Real copy.
- **Browser surfaces:** selection, caret, scrollbars, focus rings, underline offset, tabular nums — theme them.
- **Copy:** controls name the action; errors name the problem and the recovery.

## Refuse (unless the brief earns them)

- Same-size icon+heading+text cards as page structure. Nested cards.
- Hero-metric template: big number, small label, supporting stats.
- Kickers/eyebrows above headings.
- Section numbers (01 / 02 / 03) unless sequence is the information.
- Modal for a task that needs neither interruption nor protected focus.
- Gradient text. Glass/blur as decoration.
- Colored `border-left` thicker than 1px as a costume.
- Monospace as a "technical" costume rather than for code/data.
- Unicode/emoji standing in for an icon system.
- Elevation twice: 1px border under a wide soft shadow (ghost card).
- `transition: all`.
